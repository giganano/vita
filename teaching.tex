{\color{themecolor} \large
	LSST Discovery Alliance (LSST-DA) Data Science Fellowship Program (DSFP) \par
}
\par
\noindent
\url{https://lsstdiscoveryalliance.org/lsst-discovery-alliance-programs/data-science-fellowship/}
\par\noindent
Supplements graduate eduction in astronomy by teaching essential skills for
dealing with the large data set soon to be produced by the Legacy Survey of
Space and Time (LSST).
Three week-long sessions per year focused on single topic: machine learning,
data visualization, time-series analysis, data visualization, software
development, big data.
Duties: Budget management, site planning, admissions, curriculum development,
student communication, instructor recruitment.
